# Third-party software and asset scope

This candidate uses the exact dependencies recorded in `pnpm-lock.yaml`; each dependency retains its own licence and installed notices. `third-party/dependencies.json` inventories465 installed packages and their declared terms. Available root licence/notice files are retained verbatim in `apps/web/public/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt`, also emitted in the built viewer. Twenty-four packages provide no root notice file; their declared terms are recorded, with no invented permission. These notices do not license Meridian-owned source.

The runnable demo distributes no binary model, external photograph, third-party panorama, branded SVG or external texture. Microsoft Rocketbox, the original city/street/civic kits and authored character media are intentionally not admitted. Their notices are not presented as permission for unrelated material. The public representation uses locally authored procedural shells/layout and flat texture data.

Recovered engine/style algorithms remain subject to the owner's source-rights decision. A source checksum establishes identity; it does not establish authorship or redistribution permission.

## Showcase media

The repository’s docs/media directory contains original project identity/concept artwork and flat screenshots of the hosted city. Image provenance identifies generated concepts separately from current-world captures. Screenshots do not include or distribute underlying deployed model or texture source, and do not expand any third-party asset licence. The runnable demo retains its procedural representation described above.
